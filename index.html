Section 01 / Scope & identity

Introduction & overview

Welcome to AppSprouts (“we,” “our,” or “us”). AppSprouts is an AI-powered software creation platform that lets users generate HTML5 applications and progressive web apps that can run on their devices and be added to their home screens.

This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect, how it is used and stored, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated records.

Section 02 / Collection

Information we collect

AppSprouts aims to collect only the information needed to operate accounts and manage generation credits.

  • Google account identifiers: Your email address and unique Google/Firebase user identifier.
  • Passwords: We do not store raw passwords in the AppSprouts database; authentication is handled by the authentication provider.
  • Credits and purchases: Records used to manage your available credits and verify in-app purchases.
  • Prompts and generated code: Your app-creation prompt is sent to the AI processing pipeline to generate the requested code.

Section 03 / AI & device execution

Generative AI processing

AppSprouts uses third-party AI models to generate HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from the prompts you submit.

  • Prompt processing: Prompts are routed through our server-side infrastructure to AI providers to produce the requested code.
  • Generated apps: Apps run within AppSprouts on your device. Any network activity performed by a generated app depends on the code and services it uses.
  • Shortcuts: Home-screen shortcuts are created when you choose to use that feature.
  • Reporting: If an in-app reporting feature is available, it can be used to flag broken or unexpected generated output.
Section 04 / Security

Infrastructure & data sharing

AppSprouts uses Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1 for backend processing and database storage.

  • Data in transit: Connections to supported backend endpoints use HTTPS/TLS.
  • No data brokerage: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal account details.
  • Service providers: Information may be processed by providers needed for authentication, AI generation, hosting, analytics or advertising, billing, and fraud prevention, where those services are enabled.
Section 05 / Audience

Age & children's privacy

AppSprouts is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that an account was created in violation of this policy, we will take appropriate steps to address it, including deletion where required.

Section 06 / Account & data deletion

Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your AppSprouts account from inside the app or by submitting your account email below. Once submitted, your deletion request enters our processing queue and all associated personal records are permanently purged from active systems and backups within 90 days.

90-Day Deletion Timeline: Account and data deletion requests take up to 90 days to completely verify, process, and purge across all production databases and backup archives.
Method A / In the app

Open AppSprouts, go to Account / Settings, and select Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

Method B / Web form

Enter the email address associated with your AppSprouts account below to queue your deletion request. Deletion is completed within 90 days.

  • Scope of deletion: A deletion request covers your account identifiers, profile data, generation history records, and associated credit balances, except where retention is strictly required by applicable financial, taxation, or anti-fraud laws.
  • Local app files: Deleting your cloud account records does not delete HTML apps already saved locally onto your device storage. You can delete these locally within AppSprouts or your file manager.
  • 90-day fulfillment window: To ensure complete purge across distributed caches, cloud databases, and rotating backups, full eradication is completed within 90 days of request receipt.
Account Deletion Request Form

Enter the email address linked to your AppSprouts account. Once submitted, your request is logged and scheduled for permanent deletion within 90 days.

Your request will be recorded and queued for the 90-day deletion cycle.
